Retinal OCT

KineoLabs, 2026 to present

I lead the software/hardware design of a line scan laser retinal OCT imager. Shown below are device prototypes, and simulated renderings from my digital twin (because I don’t want my real biometric data circulating the internet). I am developing this digital twin to run multiphysics simulations and develop brand new imaging modalities through reinforcement-learning and Bayesian optimization.
